Why do we have two pairs of eyes
and not one,
when yours are searching for yourself
and mine are searching for myself,
yet both are searching
for the same soul?

Why do we have two hearts
and not one,
when yours beats within you
and mine beats within me,
yet both beat only to fall silent one day,
so that we may meet
and remain together forever?

Who told you that you and I
are different people,
when you are one fruit
and I am another,
yet both of us have grown
from the same roots?
